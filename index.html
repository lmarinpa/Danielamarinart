Casa Cara Blanca

Every home holds a story waiting to be told. For one family, I painted theirs: pieces made for the shape and light of their house, and for the people who live in it.

Client
Private family residence
Year
2026
Works
6 works, acrylic on canvas and sculptural relief on wood
With
Interior designer / architect
Role
Concept, proposals, renderings, painting, delivery

The works

Six pieces for one house
IThe brief

A house, waiting

The family was redesigning their home with an interior designer. The rooms were taking shape, but the walls were still silent.

Read moreClose

They didn't want art that could hang anywhere. They wanted pieces that belonged to this house and meant something to them.

So the work had three jobs: fit the architecture, carry the family's story, and earn their trust before a single brushstroke.

IIYour role

One hand, start to finish

I led the work from the first conversation to the moment each painting went up on the wall.

Read moreClose
  • Developed the concept that ties each piece to the family
  • Proposed a range of works for the family to choose from
  • Staged the chosen pieces in renderings of the real rooms
  • Coordinated with the interior designer / architect
  • Painted and delivered every piece

IVKey insights

What this taught me

  1. 01

    Choosing from real options was easier for the family than describing what they wanted from scratch.

  2. 02

    Seeing the work in renderings of their own rooms built trust before any painting existed.

Read moreClose
  1. 03

    Art and interiors work better designed together than added at the end.

  2. 04

    Meaning matters as much as fit: the pieces had to be about this family, not only sized for this wall.

VIIReflection & next steps

Looking back

What you're proudest of, and what was hardest.

Read moreClose

What I'd add now, with user-centered design methods:

  • A follow-up conversation with the family months later, to learn how they live with the work
  • Observing how each room is actually used before choosing sizes and placement
  • Testing two or three rendering options earlier, to see which reactions predict the final choice
